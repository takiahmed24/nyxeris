Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 5, 2026 • Effective Date: September 5, 2026

🛡️
Whop App Store & GDPR / CCPA Certified Compliance
We strictly safeguard merchant and customer fulfillment data. No personal data is ever sold, rented, or repurposed for advertising.

1. Introduction & Overview

CJdropshipping Fulfillment for Whop ("we", "our", or "the App") operates as a specialized dropshipping and physical order fulfillment bridge developed for merchants utilizing the Whop commerce platform. This Privacy Policy discloses our operational practices regarding the collection, processing, transmission, and storage of merchant and customer data when you install and interact with our application.

2. Information We Collect

To facilitate automated supply chain synchronization and fulfillment dispatch, the App collects the following categories of information:

🏢 Merchant Business Information
  • Whop Company Identifier (company_id)
  • Whop User / Merchant Identifier
  • Connected CJ Dropshipping API credentials (securely hashed)
  • Configured shipping preferences & SKU mapping rules
📦 Customer Fulfillment Data
  • Customer Recipient Name & Contact Email
  • Physical Delivery Address (Street, City, Postal Code, Country)
  • Recipient Phone Number (strictly for courier delivery dispatch)
  • Purchased Item SKUs, Quantities, and Product Titles

3. Purpose & Usage of Data

Collected data is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Fulfillment Execution: Transmitting order payloads to CJ Dropshipping's automated warehouses for picking, packing, customs clearance, and global courier dispatch.
  • Logistics Tracking: Continuously polling parcel tracking numbers and delivery milestones, then updating your Whop order timeline and notifying customers in real-time.
  • Inventory & SKU Resolution: Synchronizing warehouse stock balances to prevent overselling on your Whop storefront.
  • System Diagnostics: Monitoring error logs and webhook health to maintain 99.9% platform availability.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Data Disclosure

We only disclose data to verified third-party infrastructure providers necessary to fulfill your dropshipping orders:

CJ Dropshipping (Hangzhou CJDropshipping Co., Ltd.)

Order delivery details and item specifications are transmitted via authenticated HTTPS REST API (Open API 2.0) to CJ Dropshipping for international warehousing, freight shipping, and postal carrier handoffs.

Whop Inc. (whop.com)

All merchant billing transactions, app subscriptions, and storefront customer checkouts are processed securely under Whop's PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant infrastructure.

5. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We implement rigorous enterprise-grade security protocols:

  • All communications are encrypted in-transit using TLS 1.3 / SSL.
  • Webhook events from Whop are validated using HMAC SHA-256 signatures to eliminate spoofing and unauthorized injections.
  • API tokens and sensitive credentials are encrypted with AES-256 in isolated SQLite data storage.
  • We conduct regular vulnerability audits and sandbox testing.

6. Data Retention & Merchant Deletion Rights

Under GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act), merchants and customers retain full sovereignty over their data:

  • Account Disconnection: Merchants can revoke API access and disconnect their store anytime via Settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may request full deletion of order history and account metadata by contacting our privacy officer at support@nyxeris.com. Requests are completed within 72 business hours.
