Dropshipping Sourcing & White-Label Strategy for Nyxeris

To ensure zero third-party or Chinese factory branding reaches your customers, follow these proven sourcing configurations for CJ Dropshipping and top-tier alternatives.

1. CJ Dropshipping (Your Primary)

Selected

CJ Dropshipping is ideal for hardware and lifestyle gear due to US/EU local warehouses and custom packaging capabilities.

Nyxeris White-Label Checklist for CJ:

  • Enable Blind Dropshipping: In CJ Settings > Order Settings, check "Do Not Include Invoice/Chinese Flyers".
  • Custom Packaging: Use CJ's "Custom Packaging" feature to create custom Nyxeris poly-bags or custom branded cards.
  • One-Click CSV Fulfillment: Click "Export CJ Dropshipping CSV" in the Orders tab and upload it directly into CJ's "Import Excel Orders" screen.
  • Shipping Line: Select CJPacket Ordinary / Fast Line or US Domestic warehouse (3–7 day delivery).

2. Top Best Alternatives for Premium Gear

Zendrop (Best for US Warehouses & Speed)

Offers guaranteed 2–5 day shipping from vetted US suppliers. Automatically inserts a custom Nyxeris Thank You Card with discount codes into every box.

Spocket (Best for US/EU Artisans & Tech)

80% of products are based in North America and Europe. Guarantees neutral packaging and custom Nyxeris branded invoices.

Private Sourcing Agents (Wiio / HyperSKU / 3PL)

Once a Nyxeris product reaches 5+ sales per day, private agents inspect quality, store buffer inventory, and print custom embossed Nyxeris boxes with 5–8 day air freight.
